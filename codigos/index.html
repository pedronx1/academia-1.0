Our History


The Beginning

The idea of creating our own gym started with a simple conversation between two friends. We both shared an interest in training and wanted to create something that was more than just a place to exercise. We imagined a place where people could feel motivated, challenged and comfortable while working toward their goals.


The First Challenges

Creating the gym was not as easy as we first imagined. We had to save money, plan our ideas and make difficult decisions along the way. There were moments when we doubted whether our project would really work, but every problem taught us something new and made us more determined to continue.


A New Perspective

During this journey, we learned that mistakes are not necessarily failures. They can be opportunities to learn and improve. We started to understand that building something takes time, patience and the courage to change our plans when necessary.


Why "Our Gym"?

After thinking about different names, we chose "Our Gym" because the project was created by both of us. The name represents the idea that the gym does not belong only to its owners. It also belongs to the people who train here, improve themselves and become part of our story.


"This is only the beginning o our story. Our Gym was created from an idea between two friends, but its future will be built together with everyone who walks through our doors."
